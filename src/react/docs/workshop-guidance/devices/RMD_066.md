# 066: Store app deployment

**Implementation Effort:** Low to Medium

**User Impact:** Low to Medium

## Overview
Store app deployment through Microsoft Intune is the standard way to deliver public iOS/iPadOS apps to users. In a Zero Trust device program, app deployment matters because security policies are only effective when users have the approved apps they need for mail, collaboration, browser access, and business workflows.

Administrators should treat app deployment as part of the access design, not as a disconnected packaging task.

## Implementation Guidance
1. Add the public App Store app from the **Microsoft Intune admin center** and verify the correct publisher, bundle identifier, and minimum supported OS version.
2. Decide whether the app is **required**, **available for enrolled devices**, or targeted to a smaller group for pilot validation first.
3. Apply **app protection**, **app configuration**, or both where the app supports them. Deployment alone does not protect organizational data.
4. If the app is license-managed through Apple business purchasing, use the current **Apple location token** workflow rather than older VPP terminology in your operational documentation.
5. Test installation, update behavior, sign-in experience, and what happens when the app is removed or access is revoked.
6. Keep the Company Portal experience clean so users can tell which apps are mandatory versus optional.
