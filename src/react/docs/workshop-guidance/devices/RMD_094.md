# 094: LoB app Deployment

**Implementation Effort:** Medium  
**User Impact:** Medium

## Overview
Line-of-business app deployment in Intune covers internally developed or organization-specific mobile apps that are not simply consumed as public store apps. These apps often carry higher business criticality because they support internal workflows, field operations, or direct access to private business services.
For Zero Trust, LOB apps should be distributed through the most governable path available, combined with secure authentication, app configuration, and strict data-handling controls. For Android, many organizations should evaluate private apps in Managed Google Play before defaulting to raw package deployment.

## Implementation Guidance
1. Decide whether the app should be distributed as a private Managed Google Play app or as a direct Intune line-of-business package. Prefer the more supportable lifecycle when either option is viable.
2. Ensure the package is consistently signed and versioned. Changing signing approach midstream can break update paths and force reinstallation.
3. Document dependencies such as required certificates, private network access, minimum OS level, managed app configuration, and supported device modes.
4. Use phased rollout and a rollback plan for every release, especially if the app is business critical or used by frontline workers.
5. Pair the app with Conditional Access, app protection, or compliant-device requirements when the app handles sensitive corporate data.
6. Track crashes, install failures, and version adoption so app owners and endpoint admins can respond before operations are disrupted.
