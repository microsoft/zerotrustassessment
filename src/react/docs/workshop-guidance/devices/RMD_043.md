# 043: Require Play Integrity Verdict (formerly SafetyNet)

**Implementation Effort:** Low – Built-in conditional launch setting in Intune App Protection Policies for Android.

**User Impact:** Low – Transparent to users on unmodified devices; blocks or wipes corporate app data on compromised or tampered devices.

## Overview

Google replaced the legacy *SafetyNet Attestation API* with the **Google Play Integrity API**. In Microsoft Intune App Protection Policies (APP) and Android Compliance Policies, the **Play Integrity Verdict** setting verifies the integrity of Android devices before allowing access to corporate data.

### Important October 31, 2026 Enforcement Update
Google updated the definition of **Strong Integrity** (`MEETS_STRONG_INTEGRITY`) for devices running **Android 13 and later**, requiring both hardware-backed security signals and a **security update within the past 12 months**. Microsoft Intune enforces this updated definition starting **October 31, 2026**:
- Devices on Android 13+ without a security patch in the last 12 months are downgraded from *Strong Integrity* to *Device Integrity* (`MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY`), which can trigger conditional launch blocks or mark devices noncompliant.
- Admins should pair Play Integrity checks with the **Min OS version** and **Min patch version** conditional launch settings (and **Minimum security patch level** in MDM compliance policies) to warn users before they are blocked.
