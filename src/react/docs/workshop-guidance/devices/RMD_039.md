# 039: Screen capture and Google Assistant

> **Implementation Effort:** Medium  
> **User Impact:** Medium

## Overview
On Android, organizations may need to reduce the risk that protected app content is exposed through screen capture, digital assistant surfaces, previews, or other convenience features that can relay sensitive data outside the managed app boundary. These settings are particularly relevant for users who handle confidential or regulated data on personally owned devices.

In Zero Trust, this is another way of limiting unintended disclosure paths even after the user has been authenticated.

## Implementation Guidance
1. Determine which user groups truly need restrictions on screenshots and assistant-style surfaces. Not every population requires the strictest setting.
2. In the Microsoft Intune admin center, review the Android app protection policy data protection settings related to screen capture and assistant exposure.
3. Apply stricter restrictions to high-sensitivity groups first, then evaluate broader rollout.
4. Test protected workflows such as viewing email, Teams chat content, and documents in supported apps.
5. Confirm whether the restriction affects legitimate support, accessibility, or training use cases.
6. Communicate clearly that the control is designed to protect organizational data, not to monitor personal use.

These controls are most valuable when combined with copy/paste, save-as, approved keyboard, and browser protections to create a coherent managed-app boundary.
