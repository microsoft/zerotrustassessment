# 068: VPP app deployment

**Implementation Effort:** Medium

**User Impact:** Low to Medium

## Overview
Apps purchased through **Apple Business Manager** can be synchronized to Microsoft Intune using **location tokens** so the organization can assign and reclaim app licenses in a controlled way. This is the modern evolution of what many administrators still call VPP.

In a Zero Trust design, managed business app licensing helps ensure users receive approved apps through an auditable, supportable workflow rather than through unmanaged personal purchase patterns.

## Implementation Guidance
1. Connect Apple Business Manager locations to Intune using the appropriate **location token** and document which purchasing location each token represents.
2. Synchronize purchased apps into Intune and confirm the app, platform, and available license counts are correct.
3. Assign apps according to business need: required, available, or pilot-only. Use smaller groups first for validation.
4. Decide whether apps should update automatically and whether license reclaim should occur at app removal or user offboarding.
5. Coordinate with procurement or Apple administrators so token renewal and app purchasing are not disconnected from Intune operations.
6. Keep terminology current in your documentation: use **Apple Business Manager location tokens** instead of relying only on the retired VPP label.
