# 058: FLW / Shared Devices

**Implementation Effort:** High

**User Impact:** High

## Overview
Frontline and shared-device deployments have different priorities than knowledge-worker laptop and phone deployments. The goals are usually fast sign-in, predictable task-oriented app access, controlled local data retention, and low-friction handoff between shifts. In Microsoft Intune, this often means choosing a supported **shared**, **dedicated**, or **corporate-owned** enrollment pattern rather than reusing a standard single-user BYOD design.

In a Zero Trust model, frontline devices should still enforce identity, device state, app protection where supported, and minimal local data persistence.

## Implementation Guidance
1. Separate your frontline scenarios into categories: **shared sign-in devices**, **dedicated task devices**, **Teams meeting or specialty devices**, and **individually assigned corporate devices**.
2. For Android, prefer **Android Enterprise** enrollment models that match the use case, such as dedicated devices, corporate-owned work profile, or other supported corporate-owned options. Do not base new designs on legacy Android device administrator management.
3. For Apple shared scenarios, decide whether the correct design is **Shared iPad**, **shared device mode for supported apps**, or a standard assigned-device model.
4. Define the user session model: global sign-in/out, inactivity timeout, local cache removal, required apps, and whether frontline workers need self-service access through Company Portal or a limited launcher experience.
5. Use Intune to deploy required apps, configuration, compliance, and where applicable **Remote Help**, **Managed Home Screen**-style experiences, or Teams-focused settings documented for the platform.
6. Pilot shift handoff carefully. Validate what remains on the device after sign-out, what requires network access, and how lost/stolen devices are handled operationally.
7. Train support staff on the difference between wiping a shared device, retiring a device, and simply clearing a work session.
