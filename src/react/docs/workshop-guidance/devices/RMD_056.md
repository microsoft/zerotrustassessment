# 056: APNS Certificate

**Implementation Effort:** Low to Medium

**User Impact:** Low

## Overview
The Apple MDM push certificate (APNs certificate) is a foundational prerequisite for managing iOS/iPadOS and macOS devices with Microsoft Intune. Without it, Apple devices cannot maintain the management channel Intune relies on for enrollment, policy delivery, and many remote actions.

From a Zero Trust perspective, this certificate is not just setup trivia; it is a critical dependency. If it expires or is replaced incorrectly, device management continuity can break and trust signals may no longer be enforced as expected.

## Implementation Guidance
1. Create the Apple MDM push certificate in the **Microsoft Intune admin center** and complete the Apple portal steps using a controlled organizational Apple account, not a personal account.
2. Record the Apple account used for the certificate and store the renewal process in operational documentation. The same Apple account should be retained for future renewals.
3. Track expiration well in advance and renew before the annual deadline. Expired APNs certificates can disrupt management communications with enrolled devices.
4. Coordinate APNs renewal with Apple enrollment administrators so no one mistakenly creates a replacement certificate when the correct action is to renew the existing one.
5. After renewal, validate that newly enrolling devices and a small sample of already enrolled devices continue to communicate successfully with Intune.
6. Include APNs ownership in business-continuity planning, because access to the Apple portal can become a single point of failure.
